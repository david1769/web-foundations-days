About QuickNotes

Quick notes are designed to instantly capture fleeting thoughts, links, or tasks from any screen or app without opening a full note application. They serve as a temporary digital inbox that lets you jot down information quickly and organize or review it later.

How to use QuickNotes

Steps

  1. Click on the QuickNotes icon in your browser or app.
  2. Type your note in the input field.
  3. Press "Add Note" to save it.
  4. Review your notes later in the QuickNotes interface.

Features

  • Rich Content: Add text, web links, images, tags, and mentions.
  • Contextual Links: Automatically suggest adding active website URLs (like Safari) or app links. When you revisit those sites later, a thumbnail of your note reappears.
  • Handwriting Support: Draw or write by hand using an Apple Pencil on iPad.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + Shift + N Create a new note
Ctrl + Shift + F Focus the note input field
